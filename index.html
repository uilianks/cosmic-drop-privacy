Privacy Policy: Cosmic Drop

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Cosmic Drop ("the game") is a casual puzzle game for Android published by uilianks ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle.

What I collect

I do not run any server, and the game has no account system. I do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information myself.

Stored only on your device: your best scores, settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and your saved run. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Third-party services

The game uses services from Google, which may collect and process data according to their own policies:

  • Google AdMob shows a rewarded video ad when you choose to watch one for a second chance. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and where required by law, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play Games Services is used for the worldwide leaderboard. If you sign in, your Play Games player name, avatar and score are shared with Google and shown to other players on the leaderboard. If you do not sign in, the game still works and only your local top 10 is used. See https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Use your device's settings to reset or limit your advertising ID.
  • Withdraw or change ad consent in the game's privacy options where shown, or via your Google account's ad settings.
  • Sign out of Google Play Games on your device to stop leaderboard sharing.

Changes

If this policy changes I will update this page and the date above.
